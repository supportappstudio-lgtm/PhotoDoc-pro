PhotoDoc Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

PhotoDoc Pro processes your photos and documents locally on your device. Some features also use internet-loaded third-party libraries, and any advertising used in an Android release is handled as described below.

What we collect

PhotoDoc Pro does not collect, transmit, or store any of your photos, documents, or personal files on any server. We do not require account creation, sign-in, or any personal information to use the app.

Where your files are stored

  • Photos and PDFs you create are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • Your photos and documents are not uploaded to the developer's server or synced to a developer-controlled cloud service.
  • Clearing your browser data, or using "Clear All Saved Files" in Settings, permanently deletes them.

Camera & photo access

If you use the Scan Document or Passport Photo tools, the app requests access to your camera or photo library only to let you select or capture an image. That image is processed on-device and is never sent anywhere.

Third-party services

Some PDF-related tools load open-source libraries (jsPDF, PDF.js) from a public content delivery network to function. These libraries run locally in your browser and do not transmit your photos or documents to the developer.

Advertising (when enabled)

If advertising is enabled in an Android release of PhotoDoc Pro, the app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share certain information such as IP address, advertising/device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. This information is handled by Google according to its applicable policies and settings. The app's Google Play Data Safety disclosure will be updated to match the exact advertising SDK and configuration used in the released version.

PhotoDoc Pro does not use AdMob to upload or store the photos and documents that you edit in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@photodocpro.app.
